NovaCorp — Restricted
=====================

If you can read this, you are authenticated as admin. That is the whole point
of the exercise: some files are readable only by a specific account, and no
amount of cleverness as another user will open them. You have to BECOME that
user — legitimately, with their password.

Access flag:

    FLAG{p3rm1ss10n_l4dd3r_cl1mb3d}

Lesson: reused passwords are how attackers climb from one account to the next.
The admin password was sitting in a world-readable backup. Rotate credentials,
and never leave secrets in files everyone can read.
