corp-jump01 — NetOps crib sheet
===============================

This box has a leg in both networks:
  eth0  10.10.0.5   office LAN
  eth1  10.10.9.5   server segment

That is the whole point of a jump host: the office LAN cannot route to
10.10.9.0/24, so everything to the database side has to come through here.

Server-segment hosts:
  10.10.9.20  corp-db01    exports + customer database
